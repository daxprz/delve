# Enemies have health

## Summary

Enemies now have their **own health** and can be **defeated** — punches
(and shockwaves) damage them, and they vanish at 0 health.

## Definition of Done

- [x] Enemies have health and take damage.
- [x] Punches / shockwaves damage them; they're removed when defeated.
