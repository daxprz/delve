# A fourth segment: a foot at the bottom of the leg

> **BUILT AND THEN REMOVED (2026-08-14)**, at the operator's request,
> the same day. Kept as a record rather than deleted: the leg is back
> to three segments, and this says why a fourth was tried and what it
> looked like.

## What it was

A short fourth segment on the end of each leg — a tarsus lying nearly
flat so the spider rested ALONG the ground rather than balancing on
points.

Proportions were 0.11 / 0.16 / 0.58 / 0.15, keeping the third segment
far the longest so it stayed the reach; the foot was deliberately
short (0.74 against the reach's 2.92).

## Why it came out

The operator looked at it and did not want it. No fault, no bug — it
simply did not look right, which is the one thing a test cannot tell
you.

## Worth keeping from it

Adding and removing a whole segment took two small edits and needed
**no change to the body-height maths at all** — it sums each
segment's vertical contribution, so it works for any number of them.
Third time that has paid off.

If a foot is ever wanted again, the numbers above are the starting
point.
