# Sniper character (blank slate)

## Summary

The **Sniper** joins the roster: a lean humanoid with big, tall
**ears** on its head — the visual cue for the character that hears
instead of sees. Its blindness and echo-sight ship in
[STO-CHARACTER-040](./story-040-sniper-echo.md); this story is the
body and the roster entry.

## Definition of Done

- [x] "Sniper" is pickable on the character-select screen.
- [x] Its body has two large ears on the head, built procedurally
      like the rest of the body.
- [x] Slightly lower health / lighter build than the others (it is
      meant to be fragile).
- [x] Walks, jumps and works in multiplayer like any other character.
- [x] No ranged shooting yet.

## Out of scope

- Echo-sight (STO-CHARACTER-040).
- The actual long-range shooting play style — its own epic.

## Verification notes (2026-08-07)

- Two ears built on the head joint, angled outward; asserted present
  on the Sniper and absent on every other character.
- Fragile as intended: 65 health, the lowest on the roster.
