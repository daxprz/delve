# 💡 Ideas

**This is your dumping ground.** Write ideas here however they come out
— messy, half-finished, one word, whatever. Don't worry about spelling,
don't worry about which topic, don't worry about whether it's possible.

Then tell me *"check IDEAS"* and I'll turn them into proper stories and
tick them off this list.

**Nothing here is being built.** An idea is only real once it has a
STORY. That's the rule, and this file is the step *before* that.

---

## 📋 The seven big topics

Every idea in delve belongs to one of these. If yours doesn't fit any of
them, that's exciting — it means we need a new topic.

| # | Topic | What goes in it |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | 🧍 **Player character** | You. Characters, abilities, arms, bodies, moving, jumping, picking who you play as |
| 2 | 👾 **Enemies** | Everything that wants to hurt you. The spider, walkers, bosses, how they think |
| 3 | ⚔️ **Combat** | Hitting and being hit. Damage, health, dying, combos, throwing things |
| 4 | 🌍 **World** | The place you're in. Maps, levels, terrain, things to climb on, procedural generation |
| 5 | 🖥️ **UI & menus** | Screens and buttons. Pause menu, lobby, health bars, anything you read |
| 6 | 🔗 **Core** | The plumbing. Multiplayer, joining games, keeping everyone in sync |
| 7 | 🛠️ **Tools** | Things for *me*, not you. Tests, debug commands, releases |

---

## ✍️ Add your ideas below

Just add a line. That's it. Like this:

```
- the spider should scream when it loses you
- a level made of floating islands
- what if you could throw your own arm
```

You **can** put it under a topic if you know which one — but if you
don't, stick it in **Not sure yet** and I'll work out where it goes.
Working out which bucket something belongs in is my job, not yours.
